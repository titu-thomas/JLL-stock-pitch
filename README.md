# 📈 Jyothy Labs Ltd – Equity Research Stock Pitch & DCF Valuation

This project presents an independent **equity research stock pitch** on **Jyothy Labs Ltd (NSE: JYOTHYLAB)**, an Indian FMCG company with brands across Fabric Care, Home Care and Personal Care.

The project evaluates Jyothy Labs' growth prospects following the **Pril/Fa exit**, with a focus on portfolio recovery, premiumisation, distribution expansion and potential operating leverage.

The analysis combines fundamental research, segment-level revenue forecasting, a 10-year **FCFF DCF valuation**, reverse DCF analysis and valuation sensitivity analysis.

> **Valuation Date:** 11 September 2026  
> **Current Price:** ₹195  
> **DCF Value:** ₹225/share  
> **Implied Upside:** 16%

---

## 🎯 Investment Thesis

The core thesis is that Jyothy Labs can rebuild growth after the Pril/Fa exit while improving product mix and operating margins.

### 1. Pril/Fa Exit – A Reset, Not a Structural Break

The loss of Pril and Fa creates a near-term revenue reset, but the broader portfolio continues to grow.

The thesis is that Jyothy can rebuild the lost revenue through:

- Growth in Fabric Care
- Exo Liquid and other Home Care products
- Distribution expansion
- New product launches
- Geographic expansion
- Growth across existing brands

The model assumes FY27 is a transition year, followed by stronger growth from FY28.

### 2. Premiumisation Can Improve Revenue Mix

Jyothy is expanding into higher-value liquid and specialised formats across:

- Fabric Care
- Dishwash
- Insecticides

The increasing adoption of liquid and specialised products can support positive price/mix and potentially create a more profitable revenue base.

### 3. Distribution Expansion Can Drive Operating Leverage

Jyothy's expanding distribution network can support higher product availability and volumes.

Higher volumes and better manufacturing utilisation can improve fixed-cost absorption and support EBIT margin expansion.

The model assumes EBIT margin gradually improves from approximately **13% in FY26 toward ~19% over the long term**.

The reverse DCF is used to identify the level of long-term growth and profitability that is implied by the current share price.

> Reverse DCF outputs are conditional: implied growth is solved while holding other model assumptions constant, while implied margin is solved holding the revenue forecast constant.

## 🔄 Reverse DCF Analysis

Two reverse DCF scenarios are included:

### Scenario A – Implied Growth

Revenue growth is solved while holding the other DCF assumptions constant.

**Implied FY28–FY36 Revenue CAGR: 7.9%**

### Scenario B – Implied Margin

EBIT margin is solved while holding our revenue forecast constant.

**Implied FY28–FY36 EBIT Margin: 11.2%**

These scenarios are used to understand the expectations embedded in the current share price and compare them with our operating forecasts.

## ⚡ Catalysts

Key factors that could support the investment thesis include:

- **Exo Liquid scale-up** and consumer adoption
- **Recovery in margins** as crude-linked input costs stabilise
- **Continued distribution expansion**
- Growth in **liquid, premium and specialised formats**
- Recovery in Home Care following the Pril/Fa transition

---

## ⚠️ Key Risks

The principal risks to the thesis include:

- Exo Liquid failing to gain sufficient consumer adoption or distribution
- Persistently elevated crude-linked input costs
- Increased competitive intensity or a potential Pril re-entry
- Aggressive pricing, promotions or higher grammage from larger FMCG competitors
- Slower volume growth and weaker operating leverage
